# TradeFlow NG — MVP User Flow

Date: 16 September 2026

Source: requirements.md. This flow uses only CD-01–CD-05 and conditional MT-01–MT-05, all Must-have requirements. It describes a manual discovery experiment and, only if justified, one bounded manual service test. It does not establish customer demand or require software.

## 1. Actors, trigger and end state

- **Primary actor:** TradeFlow interviewer/operator, who runs and records the experiment. No external primary customer has been validated yet.
- **Secondary actors:** Actual tomato producers and people responsible for bulk tomato purchases for retail businesses in the Dikko/Niger area. Existing trader contacts may introduce them but do not automatically qualify. A counterparty, purchase approver or budget owner participates only where relevant; these roles may belong to different people.
- **TradeFlow/operator role:** Conduct phone calls or direct conversations, maintain a manual case log, review evidence, and perform and evaluate one agreed task only after the discovery gate passes. The experiment lead makes the recorded stage decision; the same person may perform both roles.
- **Starting trigger:** TradeFlow begins recruiting the founder-selected discovery group to investigate actual recent transactions. This is not an assumed customer request for a matching service.
- **Successful end state:** A traceable, case-based decision to proceed, narrow, pause or reject is recorded with supporting and contrary evidence. If a manual test occurs, its action, outcome, effort and economic response are recorded with remaining unknowns. A successful intervention additionally requires evidence that the agreed failed step was addressed; completing interviews or an introduction alone is insufficient.

Participants use phone calls or direct communication; no account, screen or application is required. The operator keeps a paper log, plain document or existing basic recording tool. “Process record” below means that manual log. Physical field verification is not a required separate step: the operator checks facts needed for the selected task with the relevant people and records what was checked, with whom and when. The requirements do not mandate visits or supporting documents.

## 2. Main successful flow

Steps 1–6 are Stage A: discovery. Steps 7–12 are Stage B and apply only if Step 6 passes. Step 13 closes the evidence review. This path assumes sufficient information, willing participants and a feasible task; exceptions appear below.

| Step | Actor | Action | Information entering the process | What the operator does | Result | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | TradeFlow operator; existing contacts where useful | Seek introductions and contact potential participants by phone or direct conversation. | Selected group: tomatoes, Dikko/Niger, organizational retailers; contact and referral details. | Record who was reached and who declined; do not treat a referral as qualification. (CD-01) | Contact attempts and responses recorded. | Step 2 for a willing contact; R1 for rejection. |
| 2 | Potential participant; operator | Establish the person's actual production or retail-business purchasing role and authority. | Actual work, location, commodity, purchasing responsibilities and authority. | Confirm whether the person fits the selected discovery group; distinguish producer, purchaser and approver roles. (CD-01) | A qualifying participant is identified without assuming customer or payer status. | Step 3 if qualified; R1 if outside the group; I1 if role cannot be established. |
| 3 | Qualified participant; operator | Reconstruct a recent transaction and a difficult one where available. | Date, role, counterparty, product/grade, quantity/unit, timing, payment terms, steps and decision-makers. | Record the actual sequence, including successful methods or no recalled failure. Do not substitute hypothetical interest for history. (CD-02) | A transaction case that can be examined. | Step 4. |
| 4 | Participant; operator | Explain the failed step, existing workaround, consequences and reasons to refuse or switch. | Recurrence and its basis; money, time, delay, rejected produce or other consequences; working alternatives and refusal reasons. | Label reports, estimates, willingly shared evidence and unknowns distinctly; never turn missing values into zero. (CD-03) | Pain, alternatives and evidence limits recorded. | Step 5; material gaps are handled through I1–I3. |
| 5 | Participant; relevant decision-maker; operator | Describe the next real sale or purchase and when action can still change it. | Requirements, original plan, last actionable moment, stock control, purchase approval and spending authority. | Record the upcoming job and roles; leave beneficiary/payer unknown unless evidenced. (CD-04) | A concrete next job and deadline, or an explicit absence/unknown. | Repeat Steps 1–5 for independent participants as needed, then Step 6. |
| 6 | TradeFlow experiment lead/operator | Compare cases and decide whether one manual test is justified. | Supporting and contradictory case records, alternatives, upcoming job, deadline and participant interest. | Record proceed, narrow, pause or reject with source references and limitations. Proceed only for a real unresolved problem, an upcoming transaction, active interest in a specific bounded task and feasible responsibilities. (CD-05) | A recorded Stage B entry decision. | Step 7 only if the gate passes; R1 for refusal/no suitable task; I1 for essential unknowns; otherwise Step 13 with a narrow/pause/reject decision. |
| 7 | Participant; operator | Agree one task and how its result will be judged. | Failed step, original plan/fallback, actionable deadline, responsibilities and what would show the step was addressed. | Record the baseline and single intervention; do not bundle checks, introductions and coordination automatically. Stop if required obligations exceed present capability. (MT-01) | One bounded test defined against the participant's existing plan. | Step 8 if feasible; otherwise R2. |
| 8 | Operator; participant; counterparty only if relevant | Check facts and obtain permissions needed for that task through direct communication. | Task-specific facts. For an introduction: authority, actual stock/need, product/grade, units, quantity, location, timing, payment/collection terms and both parties' permission. | Record what was checked, with whom and when; retain mismatches. Do not share private contacts or introduce parties without both permissions. (MT-02) | Necessary facts and permissions established for the agreed task. | Step 9 if confirmed and compatible; R1 for refusal/mismatch; I1 for failed verification or incomplete facts. |
| 9 | Operator; participating person(s) | Carry out the agreed manual task by calls/direct communication before the deadline. | Confirmed task details, permissions and current actionable deadline. | Perform only that task and log its time and participant response, including no action, refusal, missed deadline or abandonment. Record time and cash costs from the start of the test, including checks. (MT-03, MT-05) | Actual intervention and response recorded; an introduction remains an introduction. | Step 10; R2 if rejected, abandoned or too late to act. |
| 10 | Operator; participant; relevant counterparty | Follow up on what actually happened. | Subsequent actions, unresolved issues and complaints; if parties independently transact, willingly shared acceptance, timing and payment status. | Record evidence status and compare with the original plan/fallback; distinguish an order, accepted delivery and payment. Keep unavailable outcomes unknown. (MT-04) | A documented outcome and limits on attributing any benefit to TradeFlow. | Step 11; I1 if essential outcome information is unavailable. |
| 11 | Operator | Total the effort and cash cost of the manual test. | Operator/agent time and expenses for checks, actions, failures and follow-up. | Record the full effort and available cost evidence; unpaid time is still recorded. (MT-05) | A case-level cost record, with any unknowns explicit. | Step 12. |
| 12 | Actual budget owner, if identified; operator | Discuss an explicit bounded service offer once scope and beneficiary are clear. | Discovered value, alternatives, measured cost, spending authority, concrete offer and response. | Record the offer; separate interest, conditional agreement, actual agreed payment and voluntary repeat requests. Do not invent a price, assign a payer or require collection. If scope, beneficiary or budget owner remains unknown, record the commercial test as unresolved. (MT-05) | Economic response recorded, or an explicit reason it could not yet be tested. | Step 13; a refusal is recorded using R2 and does not erase the operational result. |
| 13 | TradeFlow experiment lead/operator | Review all available evidence and close this experiment cycle. | Discovery cases; gate decision; any manual action, outcomes, refusals, unknowns, costs and economic response. | Record proceed, narrow, pause or reject with references and limits. Distinguish experiment learning from service usefulness, payment evidence and repeatability. (CD-05, MT-04–MT-05 when applicable) | Traceable conclusion, including contrary evidence and unresolved hypotheses. | End this cycle; any further discovery follows the recorded decision. |

A check or an introduction is a possible task, not a compulsory product feature. No sample size, conversion target, fee or duration is imposed by this flow. Actual task details and the deadline must be discovered before the relevant action; they cannot be filled in by assumption.

## 3. Rejection / no-match path

“No match” applies only if the selected manual task involves a proposed introduction. It is not a requirement to search for or match counterparties in every case.

| Step | Actor | Action | Information entering the process | What the operator does | Result | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | Contact, participant, counterparty or operator | Decline participation/help/contact sharing, identify an ineligible contact, or identify incompatible terms for a proposed introduction. | Refusal and stated reason where provided; actual role or mismatch in stock/need, grade, units, quantity, place, timing or terms. | Record the refusal, ineligibility or mismatch at its source; do not invent a reason or count it as a successful connection. (CD-01, CD-03, CD-05, MT-02 as applicable) | A negative case or unsuitable proposed connection is visible. | R2. |
| R2 | Operator | Stop the declined or incompatible activity. | Refusal, missing permission, incompatibility, abandonment, missed deadline or rejected service offer. | Do not introduce unwilling parties, promise fulfillment or expand the agreed task. Record action status and any incurred time/cost; for an offer refusal, preserve the separate operational outcome. (CD-05, MT-01–MT-03, MT-05 as applicable) | The affected activity ends without a false success claim. | R3. |
| R3 | Experiment lead/operator | Include the negative result in the stage decision. | Recorded reason, consequences, costs and any other available cases. | Decide whether evidence supports continued discovery, narrowing, pausing or rejecting the tested offer/segment. (CD-05; MT-04–MT-05 if tested) | A documented decision rather than forced participation or a forced match. | Step 1 if continuing recruitment/discovery; otherwise Step 13. |

## 4. Failed verification / incomplete-information path

Missing customer facts are discovered or left unknown; the founder does not choose an answer on the customer's behalf. An uncorroborated customer account can still be recorded as reported: optional documents or field observation are not entry requirements.

| Step | Actor | Action | Information entering the process | What the operator does | Result | Next step |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I1 | Operator; relevant participant | Identify a missing, conflicting or unconfirmed fact, including an unavailable outcome. | The gap, its source and the step it affects: role, incident detail, deadline, authority, stock/need, terms, permission or outcome. | Label the gap explicitly and distinguish reported information from checked facts. Do not replace it with an estimate presented as fact or with zero. (CD-01–CD-04, MT-02, MT-04 as applicable) | The uncertainty and its effect on the next action are recorded. | I2. |
| I2 | Operator; person able to answer | Seek clarification through a call/direct conversation where possible. | Clarification, contradiction, inability to confirm or no response. | Record what was checked, with whom and when. Continue unaffected discovery, but hold a stage transition or manual action that depends on an essential unconfirmed fact. (CD-04–CD-05, MT-01–MT-02, MT-04 as applicable) | The fact is resolved or remains explicitly unknown. | Resume the affected main-flow step if resolved and still timely; otherwise I3. |
| I3 | Experiment lead/operator | Decide what can defensibly be concluded without the missing information. | Remaining unknowns, current deadline, any performed action and incurred costs. | Do not enter Stage B without its gate evidence or make an introduction without essential confirmed facts and permissions. If the deadline passes, record it; if the outcome cannot be learned, keep it unknown. Record a narrow/pause/reject decision or further discovery need. (CD-05, MT-02–MT-05 as applicable) | An honest incomplete/blocked result, not invented verification or claimed benefit. | Step 1–5 if further discovery is justified; otherwise Step 13. |

## 5. Completion definition

MVP completed successfully when: TradeFlow records a traceable proceed, narrow, pause or reject decision from actual discovery evidence and, if the gate passes, one bounded manual test with its actions, available outcomes, effort, economic response and remaining unknowns recorded.
