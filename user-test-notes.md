# TradeFlow Discovery — User Testing Notes

## Test Objective
Determine whether a first-time operator can understand and complete the core discovery workflow with little or no onboarding.

## Test Participants
Target: 3–5 people who were not involved in building the application. No external sessions have been conducted or results entered in this template.

## Testing protocol
1. Generate and share an application preview with an isolated practice dataset; never reset live research records for a test.
2. Recruit 3–5 people who did not build it.
3. Give tasks without identifying controls or explaining the navigation.
4. Ask for screen sharing/recording only with consent.
5. Let them attempt the task before intervening. Record any help given.
6. Observe pauses, wrong clicks, backtracking, questions, terminology confusion, failures and unexpected behavior.
7. Ask what they expected and interview them briefly afterward.
8. Record findings below. Opinions supplement observed behavior; they do not replace it.

Use practice participants and consented/synthetic audio only. AI service availability and mock/real provider status must be recorded; a mock response is not transcription accuracy evidence.
