| 1. Recruit and qualify the discovery group | Contact actual tomato producers and people responsible for bulk tomato purchases for retail businesses in Dikko/Niger through calls/direct conversations. Establish roles and authority; record who was reached, declined or did not qualify. Existing trader contacts may introduce people but do not automatically qualify. Maintain these notes in the manual case log. (CD-01) | Must have | Without actual participants from the selected group, the experiment would substitute unrelated trader opinions for evidence about the proposed customers. |
| 2. Record transaction evidence and the next actionable job | Reconstruct recent and difficult transactions where available: date, role, counterparty, product/grade, quantity/unit, timing, payment terms, steps and decision-makers. Include working methods and no recalled failure. Record failure point, workarounds, recurrence and its basis, consequences, refusal reasons and switching conditions. Distinguish reports, estimates, available supporting evidence and unknowns. Identify the next real sale/purchase, requirements, existing plan, last actionable moment, stock control, purchase approval and spending authority; leave beneficiary/payer unknown until evidenced. (CD-02, CD-03, CD-04) | Must have | These linked interview notes establish whether a real problem exists and whether anyone can act on help; a generic complaint or expression of interest is insufficient. |
| 3. Review evidence and record stage decisions | Compare independent and contradictory cases, refusals, working alternatives and missing facts. Record proceed, narrow, pause or reject with source references and limits. Enter Stage B only for a real unresolved problem, upcoming transaction, actionable deadline, active acceptance of specific bounded help and feasible responsibilities. Close the cycle with a decision informed by any test outcome, cost and economic response. (CD-05) | Must have | Prevents unsupported matching or service work and produces the MVP's required traceable learning outcome, including a valid negative result. |
| 4. Define and check one bounded manual test | After the gate passes, preserve the original plan/fallback, failed step, agreed task, responsibilities, deadline and evidence that would show the step was addressed. Check only task-specific facts and permissions; record what was checked, with whom and when. For an introduction, confirm both parties' authority, actual stock/need, grade, units, quantity, location, timing and payment/collection terms, retain mismatches and obtain both permissions before contact sharing/introduction. Hold unresolved essential facts, seek clarification where possible, and stop for incompatibility, refusal or obligations beyond capability. (MT-01, MT-02) | Must have | Conditional on Stage B. A test without a baseline, feasible scope, essential facts or required permission cannot be performed responsibly or evaluated. Manual matching is relevant only if an introduction is the chosen task. |
| 5. Perform the task and record actual outcomes | Carry out only the agreed action through calls/direct communication before the deadline. Log action, time and response, including no action, refusal, abandonment and missed deadline. Follow up on outcomes, complaints and unresolved issues; compare against the original plan without assuming causal benefit. If parties independently transact, record willingly shared acceptance, timing and payment status separately. Keep unknown outcomes unknown and preserve failed attempts. (MT-03, MT-04) | Must have | Conditional on Stage B. Without the actual intervention and follow-up there is no service test; an introduction, order, accepted delivery and payment are different outcomes. |
| 6. Measure effort and the economic response | Record operator/agent time and cash expense, including failed checks and follow-up. Once scope and beneficiary are clear, discuss an explicit bounded service offer with the actual budget owner using discovered value, alternatives and measured cost. Record the actual offer and distinguish interest, conditional agreement, actual agreed payment and voluntary repeat requests. If prerequisites are unknown, record why the commercial test remains unresolved; record rejection separately from operational outcomes. (MT-05) | Must have | Conditional on Stage B. Free participation or praise does not establish a payer or viable economics. No invented price, assumed payer, revenue target, collection obligation or payment system is needed. |
